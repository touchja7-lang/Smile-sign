# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Independent sign sellers, freelancers, and sign shop professionals. They are using this platform as a tool to generate income, calculate costs, and send quotes to their own customers. They need a tool that feels fast, reliable, and precise, not a fluffy toy.

## Product Purpose
To serve as a B2B2C order management and quotation system. Sellers order signage at a wholesale cost based on their tier, calculate their margin, and generate a white-label PDF quote for their clients. Success means the seller feels empowered, trusts the pricing, and views the platform as an indispensable piece of their business infrastructure.

## Brand Personality
Precise, professional, sharp, and trustworthy. The platform should feel like a high-end financial or developer tool (e.g., Stripe, Vercel, Linear) adapted for the physical signage industry.

## Anti-references
- Generic Dribbble UI (soft drop shadows everywhere, massive rounded corners)
- Default AI Slop (tinted cream/sand backgrounds, excessive use of standard component templates)
- Messy E-commerce (cluttered layouts, screaming badges, overwhelming colors)
- Fluffy gamification (cartoonish level bars, bouncy animations for serious business data)

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over decoration**: Every line, border, and background color must serve a structural purpose. If it's just for decoration, remove it.
- **Precision instruments**: Data, tables, and forms should feel tight and exact. Use monospaced fonts for numbers, align things rigorously.
- **High contrast, absolute colors**: Use stark white (`#FFFFFF`) or pure grayscale for backgrounds. Reserve the brand Green and Accent Yellow for highly deliberate, functional highlights (like primary actions or profit displays).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast ratios required for all text, especially critical pricing data.
- Clear structural hierarchy for scanning dense information quickly.
